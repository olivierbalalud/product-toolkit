1. Patron picks a delivery date and views that day's menu.
2. Patron selects the food items for the meal.
3. System shows the total with taxes and delivery charge, and the Patron confirms it.
4. Patron chooses a delivery window, a location and a payment method.
5. System accepts the order, reserves the items and the slot, and notifies the cafeteria.
6. System sends the Patron an order confirmation.
