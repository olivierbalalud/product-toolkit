**Title:** Publish Changes  
**Actor:** User  
**Description:**  
Click the book icon in the top left to open the assets panel. Click the "Libraries" button. A modal with your files opens. If you made changes, a blue dot is shown. Click the button that says "Publish 12 changes". Write a description in the text field if you want to. Hit the blue Publish button in the bottom right corner of the dialog. A loading spinner spins while Figma uploads to AWS S3. Once done, everyone in the company gets the update on their screens.
