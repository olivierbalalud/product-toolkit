**Title:** Social Media Scheduler  
**Actor:** Anyone  
**Description:**  
The user clicks the "Share" button at the top right of the Canva canvas. They click "Schedule". A calendar UI appears. The user picks tomorrow at 9:00 AM. Then they choose Twitter and Instagram. They paste their marketing text into the box. If they click Schedule Post, the system connects to the social media APIs using their API secret keys. The design is saved and sent out tomorrow automatically.
