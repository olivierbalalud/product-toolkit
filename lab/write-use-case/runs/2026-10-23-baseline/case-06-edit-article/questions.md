# Questions for the product owner: Edit an article

1. Who edits: any visitor, or only registered members?
2. Is the system under design the whole wiki, or only the editor?
3. Can members edit the whole article, one section, or both?
4. What is already true before the edit starts (article shown, editing enabled), and do we need to check it again?
5. Does any other person or outside system take part in an edit?
6. How often does editing happen?
7. What does each stakeholder (editor, readers, watchers, moderators) need protected or achieved?
8. What must be true however the edit ends, for the published article and for what the member typed?
9. What must be true when the save succeeds: what is recorded, and is the previous version kept?
10. Is the edit summary required, pre-filled, or optional?
11. Does the member choose to watch the article when submitting, and what happens after the save besides recording it?
12. What does preview show, and does the member carry on editing afterwards?
13. What does compare show changes against: the version they opened or the latest saved version?
14. How long do opening the editor and saving take, and does the member wait?
15. What happens if the member cancels part way?
16. What happens if their session expires before they submit?
17. What happens if someone else saved the same article while they were editing?
18. What happens if the save fails part way through?
19. What happens if the article is deleted, or protected, or the member is blocked, while they edit?
20. Are there rules or size limits the content or the summary must meet?
21. What happens if they submit without changing anything, or submit twice?
22. Can a member ask to export or erase their contributions, and is that handled here?
