# Questions for the product owner

1. Who starts a Blend: any listener, or only some plans?
2. What exactly is the system under design: which apps and back-end services?
3. Why does the listener start a Blend, and with how many people?
4. What must be true before they start (signed in, minimum listening history)?
5. Does the friend accept right away, or later, and does the listener wait for it?
6. Where does the listener's job end: at sending the link, or when the Blend appears in their library?
7. Do any outside systems take part, such as messaging apps or social networks you integrate with?
8. How can the listener send the link?
9. How often does a listener start a Blend?
10. What do the listener, the invited friend and artists or rights holders each need protected or achieved?
11. However it ends, what must be true: is anything created or shared before acceptance?
12. When it succeeds, what is true, what is still pending, and how does the listener hear about the outcome?
13. Can the listener ask to export or erase the personal data involved, and who handles that?
14. Is the link made immediately, or does it take time?
15. Can the link be used once or by several people, and who joins if several open it?
16. Is the expiration window fixed, and how long is it?
17. What happens if the listener makes a link but never sends it, or changes their mind?
18. What if they ask for a second invitation: to the same friend, or to another one?
19. Is there a cap on the number of Blends or open invitations per listener?
20. Can anyone signed in be refused an invitation?
21. What happens if the connection drops while the link is made?
22. Are expired links, used links, thin listening histories and unlicensed tracks handled here or when the friend accepts?
