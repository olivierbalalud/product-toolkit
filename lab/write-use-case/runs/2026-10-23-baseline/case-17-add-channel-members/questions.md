# Questions for the product owner: UC-017

1. Who adds colleagues: any workspace member, or only certain roles, and is their right to add people checked or assumed?
2. What makes them start: what situation leads them to add colleagues to an existing channel?
3. What must already be true before they start (signed in, channel exists, they are in the channel)?
4. Does any outside system or other person take part, such as an approver or a directory service?
5. Who can be added: only existing workspace members, or also new people and guests from other organisations?
6. Can they add one colleague at a time, or several at once?
7. How often does this happen?
8. What does each stakeholder want from it: the member, the colleagues added, the people already in the channel, the administrators?
9. However it ends, what must always be true (for example, nobody added without confirmation)?
10. When it works, what is true, and what is the member told?
11. Who decides who may add people to a channel, and what rules apply?
12. What happens if the member isn't allowed to add people to that channel, and when are they told?
13. What happens if the colleague they look for can't be found?
14. What happens if the colleague is already in the channel?
15. Can they correct a wrong pick before confirming, and how?
16. What happens if they give up before confirming?
17. What happens if a colleague can no longer be added at the moment of confirming, for example a deactivated account, and are the others still added?
18. What happens if someone else adds the same colleague at the same moment?
19. Is there a limit on how many people a channel can hold or how many can be added at once?
20. How long does adding the colleagues take: immediate, or does it run in the background?
21. Can a member ask to export or erase the personal data involved, such as their channel memberships, and is that part of this use case?
22. What priority does this use case have?
