# Grades: holdout drafts against gold

Criteria: `docs/research/use-case-quality.md` (Q1 to Q15). "Invented" counts behaviour, rules, conditions or facts absent from the gold that a product owner had not decided. Items a draft flags as "recommendation, not decided" are still counted, because they are still in the text; the paragraphs say when a draft flags them.

**Differences.** Both drafts cover the gold's skeleton: enter items, taxes with the tax calculator's failure, card authorization with decline or timeout, cash, record and forward with a store-and-forward fallback, the manager override, and cancellation before payment. A stays close to the gold. Its main error is structural: it makes customer identification a main-scenario step instead of the conditional discount path, and that creates two small extensions the gold doesn't need. It also omits the variations. B has the same coverage, including the variations and all three gold rules, but adds about 18 undecided behaviours and rules, which take up half the use case. It lists them as open recommendations, and it also branches on card or cash inside the main scenario. B's privacy answer ("no") contradicts the gold.

## Overall verdict

| Case | Better draft | Margin |
|---|---|---|
| case-04-process-sale | A | Clear. Equal coverage; A invents 3 against 18, scores one point higher on quality and has no main-scenario branching. |
| case-15-schedule-social-post | A | Moderate. B covers one point more, but invents 12 against 1 and scores lower on quality. |
| case-20-book-home | A | Moderate. B covers one point more, but invents 15 against 1, adds technology detail and gets privacy wrong. |
| case-23-connect-bank-feed | B | Clear. B is close to the gold with 2 inventions against 13, and it is better on quality and on form. |

Pattern: one method produces drafts that stay tight to what was decided, with few or no inventions. Its losses are omissions: a missing variation, a supporting actor, a validation step. In three of the four cases that method is A, and in case 23 it is B. The other method brainstorms widely. It reaches gold coverage or slightly above, but adds 12 to 18 undecided items per case (most of them flagged as recommendations), answers privacy "no" against the gold's `deferred` in every case, and puts more weight on form issues.
